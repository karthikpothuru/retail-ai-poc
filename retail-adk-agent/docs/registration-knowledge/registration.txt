Phase 8.1 POC registration: send POST /api/v1/customers with application/json and the email, givenName and familyName fields. For synthetic demo data, use Asha, Rao and asha.rao@example.test. The service trims names and normalizes email by trimming and lowercasing it. A successful registration returns HTTP 201 with the generated customer UUID, email, names and a Location header. Registration does not send an email verification link or require OIDC.
